Corrections Policy

ACCOUNTABILITY & AUDIT TRAIL

Technical accuracy is foundational to Machine Report World. When an error in specifications, dates, model designations, or market attribution is identified, we correct it promptly and transparently.

How Corrections Are Handled

  • Substantive Factual Corrections: If an article contains a material error in machine specifications, GVW ratings, pricing, or launch dates, the text is updated and an explicit revision entry is logged in the article's permanent updateHistory audit trail.
  • Clarifications & OEM Updates: When a manufacturer updates a specification brochure or clarifies regional availability after initial publication, we update the story dossier and note the revision date.

Report an Error

Readers, OEM engineers, and company spokespeople can submit a correction request directly to our editorial review queue with supporting documentation or brochure links.
