Privacy Policy

DATA MINIMIZATION

MACHINEWIRE collects only the minimum information required to deliver industry news briefings and review editorial submissions.

1. Information We Collect

  • Newsletter Subscriptions: When you subscribe to the MACHINEWIRE Briefing, we record your email address, chosen segment, and consent timestamp.
  • Editorial Submissions: When you submit a news tip or correction via our contact form, we store the contact details and source links you provide for editorial verification.
  • Admin Authentication: Strictly necessary HTTP-only session cookies (`bm_session`) are used solely for authenticated editorial staff.

2. How Data Is Used & Protected

We do not sell, rent, or trade subscriber email addresses or contributor contact details to third parties. To request removal of your email from the briefing list, contact the editorial desk via our contact page.
