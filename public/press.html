EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES & COMMUNICATIONS

Machine Report World is the independent editorial publication covering global construction machinery, agricultural equipment, and commercial vehicle engineering.

Editorial Principles

  • Zero Invented Data: We never generate fictional horsepower, operating weight, breakout force, or pricing data.
  • Primary Sources: Editorial coverage cites official manufacturer press releases, homologation documents, and technical papers.
  • Editorial Independence: Inclusion in our directories does not imply commercial endorsement or OEM sponsorship.
  • Transparent Corrections: Material corrections are noted with timestamps on updated articles.
